# "Add to How to Be Cool" share button (iPhone)

Lets you tap **Share** on any TikTok, Instagram or website post and send it straight to the guide's **+ Add** form.

## One-time setup (about 3 minutes)

1. **Sign in on your phone first.** Open **howtobecoolclub.com** in **Safari**, scroll to the bottom, use **Owner sign-in**, and tap the link in your email.
2. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **+** to make a new shortcut.
3. Tap the name at the top and rename it **Add to How to Be Cool**.
4. Tap the **ⓘ** (or the down arrow next to the name → **Details**) and turn on **Show in Share Sheet**. Tap **Done**.
5. At the top it now says *Receive __Any__ input from __Share Sheet__*. Tap **Any** and keep **URLs**, **Text** and **Safari web pages** checked.
6. Add these three actions (use the search bar at the bottom):
   1. **URL Encode** — it should say *URL Encode __Shortcut Input__*. (Tap the blue word to pick **Shortcut Input** if needed.)
   2. **Text** — type `https://howtobecoolclub.com/?add=` and then, right after it with no space, tap **URL Encoded Text** in the suggestions bar above the keyboard to insert it.
   3. **Open URLs** — it should say *Open __Text__*.
7. Tap **Done**.

## Using it

- **TikTok:** on a video tap **Share** → swipe the bottom row to **More** (or **Share to**) → **Add to How to Be Cool**.
  The guide opens with the link, caption, creator and thumbnail filled in. Pick a category and add it.
- **Instagram:** tap the **paper plane** → **Share to…** / **More** → **Add to How to Be Cool**.
  Instagram doesn't share post details with other sites, so you'll get the link and type a name and category.
- **Any website in Safari:** **Share** → **Add to How to Be Cool**, and the page's details and photo fill in like "Fill from link".

The first time, you may need to tap **Edit Actions** at the bottom of the share sheet and add the shortcut to your **Favorites** so it's near the top.

## If something's off

- **It says to sign in:** open howtobecoolclub.com in Safari and sign in (step 1), then share again.
  A home-screen bookmark of the site keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so sign in in Safari too.
- **Nothing filled in:** the post may be private, or the site may block outside readers. Fill it in by hand.
